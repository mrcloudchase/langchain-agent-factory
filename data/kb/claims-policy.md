# Claims policy

## Coverage
Sudden and accidental events are covered: burst pipes, fire, theft with a police report, storm damage with a dated weather record.

## Exclusions
Gradual deterioration, wear and tear, and damage noticed over a period of months are excluded. Claims without a dated incident are excluded.

## Auto-approval limit
Claims up to $2,500 with complete documentation may be approved by the agent. Claims above $2,500 are referred to an adjuster even when covered.

## Documentation
Every claim needs a dated incident description and supporting evidence: invoice or estimate, photos, and for theft a police report. Missing documentation means refer, not deny.

## Decisions
Deny only on an explicit exclusion. When unsure, refer. Every recorded decision is confirmed by a human.
