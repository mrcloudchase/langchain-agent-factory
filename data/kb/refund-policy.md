# Refund policy

## Duplicate charges
A duplicate charge is refunded in full once the billing system shows two captures for the same period. No manager approval is needed, but every refund is executed through the approval queue so a human confirms the amount.

## Annual plans
Annual plans are refundable in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days they are not refundable; offer a prorated credit toward a future renewal instead and escalate if the customer insists.

## Limits
Support agents may refund up to $500 per order. Larger amounts are escalated to tier 2.
