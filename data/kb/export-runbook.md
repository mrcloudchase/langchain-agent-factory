# Reports export runbook

## Known issue: CSV export spinner
Since release 4.12 the CSV export hangs for workspaces with more than 50,000 rows. Workaround: ask the customer to filter to a date range under 90 days, or export as XLSX. Engineering ticket ENG-2210 tracks the fix.

## Escalation
Escalate to tier 2 if the workaround fails or the workspace is on the Team plan or higher.
