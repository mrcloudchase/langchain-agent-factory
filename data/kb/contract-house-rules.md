# Contract house rules

## Data use
Vendors may not use customer data to train models or build derived datasets without separate written consent. Aggregated, de-identified use requires opt-in.

## Termination
All licenses and rights granted to a vendor end when the agreement ends. No perpetual or survival licenses to customer or derived data. Mutual termination on 30 days or more written notice is acceptable.

## Notice
Notices must be in writing to the named contact. Email counts if the contract says so.

## Liability
Liability caps below 12 months of fees need legal sign-off. Unlimited liability for data breaches is preferred.
